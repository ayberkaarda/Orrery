Orrery

A desktop physics laboratory that brings space dynamics, aerodynamics and atmospheric entry into one application and couples them.

Observable, verifiable, deterministic. A tool, not a game.

Download 0.1.0 Source on GitHub

Windows x64 developed and tested · Linux x86_64 built in CI, packages untested · macOS out of scope

The orrery here is drawn in your browser: circular orbits paced by Kepler's third law. Drag to turn it, select a planet to follow it. Every image below is rendered by Orrery itself.

One chain, three domains. Atmosphere models and a drag coefficient table sampled from the aerodynamic solver drive a probe's entry, stepped by the same integrators as the Space workspace.

01 · Workspaces

Three workspaces, one instrument

Space, Aero and Entry each have their own main bar, docked panels and camera. Physics runs on its own threads and reaches the renderer through immutable snapshots, so no frame waits for a solver. Every run can be saved as an experiment, listed, compared and opened again.

ISpace dynamics

Space

N-body gravity with symplectic and adaptive integrators, black holes, collisions and tidal disruption.

  • ExperimentsThe J2000 solar system, Sun-Earth-Moon, the three-body figure eight, Sgr A* with the star S2 and a lone black hole.
  • IntegratorsLeapfrog (kick-drift-kick, default), Yoshida 4th order, IAS15 (adaptive) and RK4 for comparison; direct summation or a Barnes-Hut octree.
  • Black holesPaczynski-Wiita potential for the dynamics; a ray-marched Schwarzschild lensing pass and a thin accretion disk with Doppler beaming and gravitational redshift.
  • Time warpFrom 1x to 1e7x, held inside a time-warp budget.
  • PanelsBody Inspector (osculating orbit), System Monitor (energy drift, step time, warp), Experiment, Render Settings.

IIAerodynamics

Aero

Flow around a chosen or imported 3D body, with three solvers for three regimes: force coefficients, pressure maps and flow fields.

  • Solvers2D and 3D panel method; modified Newtonian theory with a Sutton-Graves stagnation heat flux estimate; lattice Boltzmann D2Q9 and D3Q19 with a Smagorinsky subgrid model.
  • RegimesFlow conditions give the Reynolds and Mach numbers; the regime selector recommends a solver and warns outside its validity range without blocking the run.
  • GeometryPrimitives from spheres and wedges to Apollo and Orion type capsules and NACA 4-digit sections and wings; OBJ, STL, glTF/GLB, PLY and FBX import from disk or an https URL, with limits, a SHA-256 cache and automatic repair.
  • ResultsCd, Cl, Cm with a breakdown and convergence plots; Cp on the body, force arrows, section planes, streamlines, particles and the Q-criterion iso-surface. Compared by angle of attack, exported as CSV.

IIIAtmospheric entry

Entry

Atmosphere models and a drag table sampled from the aerodynamic solver drive a probe through the atmosphere of Earth or Mars.

  • AtmospheresUS Standard Atmosphere 1976, a mean Mars profile (NASA Glenn fit) and exponential atmospheres.
  • ProbeConstant mass, no lift, with a Cd(Mach) table edited by hand or sampled from the Newtonian solver; IAS15, Yoshida 4 or leapfrog for the gravity part of the step.
  • ProfileAltitude, speed, g-load, dynamic pressure and heat flux against time; the ground track; a 3D trajectory coloured by g-load or heat flux.
  • OutcomesLanded, skip-out or orbiting.

02 · Trust

Built to be checked

Orrery shows its working. Runs are reproducible, simplifications are written down where they live, and the numbers it reports are compared against references in the test suite.

Determinism

Bit-identical stepping

A fixed-timestep accumulator with integral time keeping: the state after N steps is bit-identical whatever the frame rate. A state hash checks it for any frame pacing, with the time-warp budget active too.

Time-warp budget

Honest about speed

Physics keeps to a share of wall-clock time. When a requested warp does not fit, it drops to the highest level that does and says so: a LIMITED chip, and the requested and achieved warp in the System Monitor. It recovers when the load drops, with hysteresis.

Approximations

Simplifications on record

Every physical simplification is marked in the code and listed in docs/physics.md. A checker keeps the formula index, its source files and its tests in step, and fails CI when a marked file has no entry.

Threads

Solvers never stall a frame

Physics runs on its own threads and hands the renderer immutable snapshots. The core libraries never link OpenGL, GLFW or Dear ImGui; a CI check enforces the boundary.

Continuous integration

Three compilers, sanitizers

Every pull request builds and tests with GCC 13, Clang 17 and MSVC, runs AddressSanitizer and UBSan, clang-format, clang-tidy and cppcheck. A nightly build with link-time optimisation re-checks the determinism hashes.

Errors

No silent failure

Errors appear where they happen: the status line, the panels, a modal for import errors. Unknown configuration keys are reported with their line; a fatal error writes its reason to a crash log.

Build from source

CMake 4.1 or newer, Ninja, a C++20 compiler (MSVC 2022, GCC 13 or Clang 17) and vcpkg in manifest mode, which fetches GLFW, glad, Dear ImGui, ImPlot, glm, Eigen, assimp, libcurl, miniz, oneTBB, nlohmann/json, toml++ and Catch2.

Windows · Developer PowerShell (x64) 
$env:VCPKG_ROOT = "C:\path\to\vcpkg"
cmake --preset release
cmake --build --preset release
ctest --preset release
Linux · shell 
export VCPKG_ROOT=/path/to/vcpkg
cmake --preset linux-release
cmake --build --preset linux-release
ctest --preset linux-release
Run 
orrery_app                                   # the window, solar system
orrery_app --experiment figure_eight         # a bundled experiment by name
orrery_app --headless --experiment black_hole --out bh.png --size 1920x1080
orrery_app --headless --experiment entry_apollo_like --out entry.png

Press F1 in the window for the shortcuts. --headless renders one PNG offscreen and never shows a window; without an OpenGL 4.6 driver it exits with code 3.

04 · Status

Status and limitations

Version 0.1.0. What has run where, and what the models leave out, stated plainly.

  • Windows · MSVC 2022Tested

    Developed and tested here: the window, the GPU tests and the packaged application have run.

  • Linux · GCC 13, Clang 17CI only

    The headless build and its tests run in CI and the windowed build compiles, but CI runners have no OpenGL: the window and GPU tests have never run on Linux, and the tarball and AppImage are untested.

  • macOSOut of scope

    No OpenGL 4.6.

  1. No solver is validated CFD. The panel method is inviscid (skin friction is a separate flat-plate estimate), Newtonian theory is meant for Mach 5 and above, and lattice Boltzmann is a low-Mach method; each warns outside its range.
  2. Black holes are Schwarzschild only, without spin; the dynamics near them use a pseudo-Newtonian potential, not general relativity.
  3. Entry is simplified: constant mass, no lift, no rotation, no ablation. The heat flux is a stagnation-point correlation for display.
  4. The J2000 solar system is ten point masses from JPL Horizons state vectors: no asteroids, oblateness or relativity.
  5. The Aero workspace runs lattice Boltzmann on the CPU; large 3D lattices are slow there. During the onset of vortex shedding the GPU and CPU solutions differ by up to 7.1e-4 (relative L2), above the 1e-4 criterion.
  6. Performance figures come from one machine (AMD Radeon RX 9060 XT).
  7. Planned items not in V1: bloom, Hill spheres and ghost trajectories, a Rayleigh and Mie atmosphere; see docs/limitations.md. Fluids, thermal and waves (V2) and later domains are a roadmap, not code.
  8. Imported models are not validated, and the licences of downloaded models are not checked.
