Orrery
Physics laboratory: space dynamics, aerodynamics and atmospheric entry.

This package contains the Orrery application, its bundled experiments and
scenarios, and the runtime libraries the application needs.

The third-party libraries in this package are distributed under their own
licences. Their notices are installed with the application in
share/doc/orrery/third_party, one folder per library.
